The idea

One surface.
Everything you
glance at.

Your desktop doesn't need another window. It needs a place for the things you check fifty times an hour — and a way to dismiss them just as fast.

Ostrov pins a single pill to the top edge. When something is happening, it grows. When it's done, it melts back into the bezel.

FAQ

The short answers.

Which versions of Windows work?

Ostrov targets Windows 10 and 11 on 64-bit. It relies on WebView2, which ships with Windows 11 and is usually present on updated Windows 10 installs.

Do I need an account?

No. Install, launch, and the island is there. There's no sign-in step in the app.

Why does SmartScreen warn me?

The current build isn't code-signed, so Windows may ask you to confirm before running it. You can verify it against the published SHA-256 hashes first.

How do I remove it?

Open Settings → Apps → Ostrov and choose Uninstall, or use the "Uninstall Ostrov" shortcut in the Start menu folder.

What's it built with?

A Tauri shell around a WebView2 surface, packaged with a per-user installer. That's why the whole thing lands under 13 MB.

Does it interfere with my apps?

The island is an always-on-top overlay, not a fullscreen window. It sits above your apps and collapses back to the edge when there's nothing to show.
